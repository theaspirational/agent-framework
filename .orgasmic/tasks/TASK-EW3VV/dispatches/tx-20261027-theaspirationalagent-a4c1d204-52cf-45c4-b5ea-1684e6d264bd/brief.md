DECISIONS ALREADY MADE BY THE OWNER (implement; do not revisit)
1. A FIFTH ERROR CODE: `connection-failed`. The strict dial's fixed codes become: provider-threw | provider-empty | credential-unusable | engine-unsupported | connection-failed. `connection-failed` is for DNS failure, connection refused, TLS failure, the open timeout, and socket errors after a successful open. `credential-unusable` stays only for real token problems: absent, empty, unusable, or refused by the server.
2. A MINIMUM CREDENTIAL FORMAT: in strict mode a provider result shorter than 32 characters is refused with a clear fixed error that does not include the value (or its actual length). No other rule is added (no character-set rule beyond the existing headerCarries(), no entropy rule). The refusal reports under credential-unusable. 32 is a length floor, not proof of randomness; say so in the README.
